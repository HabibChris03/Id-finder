Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our services

How long does it take to find a lost ID once it's reported?

The time varies depending on when and where the ID was lost. Once reported, our system immediately checks for matches in our database. If someone has already found and registered your ID, we can connect you within 24 hours. Otherwise, we'll notify you as soon as a match is found.

Is there a fee for using ID Finder services?

No, ID Finder is completely free for users searching for lost IDs. Our mission is to help reunite people with their important documents without any cost. We may offer premium verification services in the future, but basic search and reporting will always remain free.

How is my personal information protected?

We use advanced encryption (SSL/TLS) to protect all data transmitted through our platform. Your personal information is only shared with someone who has found your exact document, and only after verification. We never sell or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

What should I do if I find someone's ID card?

Please visit our Found ID page to report the found document. Fill out the form with as much detail as possible, and keep the document safe until the owner contacts you. We'll help facilitate the safe return of the ID to its rightful owner.

Do you serve all regions of Cameroon?

Yes! ID Finder serves all 10 regions of Cameroon. Our platform is accessible nationwide, and we work with local partners in major cities including Douala, Yaoundé, Bamenda, Bafoussam, Garoua, and of course Buea where our main office is located.
